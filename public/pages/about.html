About The Project

Analyze Resume

Project Purpose

AI Resume Coach is an enterprise-grade full-stack platform engineered to bridge the gap between job seekers and applicant tracking systems (ATS). By combining a transparent, rule-based ATS evaluation engine with Gemini LLM suggestions, job seekers receive honest, quantifiable, and zero-hallucination feedback to optimize their resumes and ace technical interviews.

Architecture & Tech Stack

🎨 Frontend System

Pure HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript, and an enterprise CSS Dark Design System. Fast, accessible, and responsive across all device sizes.

⚙️ Backend Architecture

Node.js and Express.js REST API providing secure file uploads via Multer, text extraction engines, and algorithmic scoring services.

📄 Document Parsers

Server-side text extraction using pdf-parse and mammoth, supporting standard PDF and DOCX resume formats.

🗄️ Database Layer

MongoDB with Mongoose ODM schemas for persistent analyses, evaluation reports, interview sessions, and contact inquiries.

🤖 Google Gemini AI

Integrated with the Google Gen AI SDK for bullet point enhancement, custom interview question generation, and answer rubric evaluations.

📊 Rule-Based ATS Engine

Configurable weights for keyword matching (30%), skills repertoire (25%), sections (15%), formatting (10%), experience (10%), and education (10%).

Responsible AI & Data Privacy

We believe in honest career preparation. Our ATS scoring algorithm does not hallucinate false qualifications or guarantee job offers. Scores are derived strictly from verifiable text extracted from your resume against targeted job descriptions.
